About Kakia Melissa

Handcrafted in every batch.

Kakia Melissa is a small brewery on Kythira, born from a couple's passion for good beer and Greek warmth. The name honors the island's legend of Kakia Melissa—a wise woman whose kindness brought people together. We brew lager the same way: with care, tradition, and the spirit of bringing good people around a table.

Our lager

A clean lager with golden depth.

Kakia Melissa Lager

Inspired by the Viennese tradition, it is brewed with four selected barley malts and two noble hop varieties. The result is a full-bodied craft beer with a warm amber color and rich malt aromas.

Kakia Melissa is bottled unfiltered, keeping all its natural ingredients intact and offering a fuller mouthfeel. It is a 100% vegan and absolutely natural product, without any additives or preservatives. At the same time, through careful, gentle pasteurization, we manage to lock in its flavor profile, ensuring stability and quality in every bottle.

  • Style: Vienna Lager
    Unfiltered, pasteurized.
  • Vol: 5.0%
  • Ingredients: Water, barley malt, hops, yeast.
  • Taste: Smooth malt sweetness, strong notes of biscuit and freshly baked bread, in perfect balance with a clean, pleasantly dry finish.
